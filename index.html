FAQ

What exactly do I get for a credit?

The person or company behind the posting, plus matching LinkedIn profiles. Sometimes we can name the individual directly, sometimes the company and the people to talk to there.

Where does this data come from?

Public job boards — the places companies post paid project work. Everything we use is published openly: the posting itself, and the professional profiles we match it to. We don't buy lists, and we don't touch anything private.

Can I get banned for contacting people directly?

We can't speak for another platform's rules — if you're active on one, read its terms and use your judgment. What we give you is who published a posting and their public professional profile. Where you take it from there is your call, and it happens outside that platform.

What if a reveal finds nothing?

No result, no credit spent. The credit isn't taken — you only spend one when we return a result.

What if I upgrade mid-week?

You get the new plan straight away. Your week starts over from that day, and any names you haven’t used are added on top.

If you downgrade, it takes effect when your current week ends.

Can my team use this too?

Yes. Every plan includes your whole team during beta — there's no per-seat pricing yet. Connect Slack and everyone sees the same leads in one place, so nobody has to forward anything.

Seats are on the roadmap. Pricing will change when they ship, and accounts opened before then will be grandfathered.

Does cold outreach actually work on these clients?

Yes, but they're leads, not guaranteed clients. These are companies with a budget and stated intent, so reply rates are far better than untargeted cold email. Some buy, most don't — same as any pipeline.

What decides it is the pitch. Agencies getting the most out of this pick one thing they're excellent at and reach out only where they're clearly the best answer. A generic proposal sent to a well-targeted lead is still a generic proposal.

How accurate is the match?

We trace each posting back through public signals and show you what we found. Sometimes that’s one named person. Sometimes it’s the company plus the people there worth talking to — in which case we show you several, because we’d rather hand you three real candidates than guess at one.

Think of it as the research done for you, not a guarantee. The last step is still a human glance: you check the profile, you decide it’s them, you write. That takes seconds instead of an hour.

If we can’t find anyone at all, the credit isn’t spent.

How fast is it?

We check for new postings every minute and send within a minute of finding one. In practice you have the lead about two minutes after it goes live.

What if the client already hired someone?

That’s often the better lead. A company that paid for this work once has budget, a habit of hiring out, and a project that’s now live and needs maintaining, extending or redoing.

Filled jobs are also proof the client actually pays — an open posting is only a stated intention.

Do I need an account on the job platforms?

No. Nothing to connect, nothing to link. You log into Leadhoundy and that’s it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You keep the plan until your current week ends, and nothing renews after that.

What if two people on my team reveal the same lead?

It costs a name each time. We can’t tell whether it’s the same person checking twice or a colleague who hasn’t seen it yet, and we’d rather charge the credit than quietly show one of you stale data.

If you’re working leads as a team, route different scanners to different channels so you’re not covering the same ground.
